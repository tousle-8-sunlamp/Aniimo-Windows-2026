## 🕹️ About

Aniimo is a free-to-play creature-catching open-world action RPG set across the vibrant continent of Idyll. Capture, collect, evolve, and battle unique Aniimo while exploring a world shaped by weather, time, environments, and player choices. Twine with your companions to become them, gaining new abilities and ways to travel through the world. This Windows build is planned for pre-release ahead of the September 16, 2026 PC launch.

Aniimo is for players who enjoy colorful open-world adventures, creature collecting, real-time combat, and transforming into their companions.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Open-World Exploration** — Discover diverse regions, encounters, and secrets across the continent of Idyll.
- 🐾 **Creature Collection** — Capture and collect unique Aniimo using Aniipods, traps, and precise timing.
